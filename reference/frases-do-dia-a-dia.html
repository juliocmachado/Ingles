Conhecer alguém · Meeting someone

Da Lição 1.

Você ouveVocê responde
Hi! I’m Emma.Hi, I’m ___. Nice to meet you.
Nice to meet you.Nice to meet you, too.
Where are you from?I’m from Brazil.
How are you?I’m good, thanks. And you?
See you around!Nice meeting you. See you!

Lembretes · Reminders

  • from vai no final: Where are you from? (não “From where are you?”).
  • meet = primeira vez · see = já se conhecem.
  • How are you? pede resposta curta e positiva — e devolva: And you?
  • Na fala, use contrações: I’m, name’s.
